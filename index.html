About Hemu

Hemu means harmony. Most of our work is women's health — the years spent trying, the months after birth, and the long turn through menopause.

A first visit takes an hour. Your physician will ask about your sleep, your appetite and your stress before deciding what to treat — because in Chinese medicine those are not side notes, they are the case.

Cycle, sleep, digestion and mood are read as one system. A difficult period and a difficult night are not separate problems here — which is why the first visit asks about all of it before anything is decided.

Acupuncture 针灸

Deep, structured Chinese bodywork for necks, shoulders and lower backs that have stopped letting go.

Cupping 拔罐

Formulas built for your case and adjusted as it changes. Taiwan-sourced, batch-tested, no guesswork.

Physiotherapy 物理治疗

Hands-on rehab and loading work for sports injuries, sprains and postpartum recovery.

Moxibustion 艾灸

Gentle warmth held over specific points — often used through the luteal phase and after birth.
